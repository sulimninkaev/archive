**Allah (swt) sagt:**

> فَلَمَّآ أَتَىٰهَا نُودِىَ مِن شَـٰطِئِ ٱلْوَادِ ٱلْأَيْمَنِ فِى ٱلْبُقْعَةِ ٱلْمُبَـٰرَكَةِ مِنَ ٱلشَّجَرَةِ أَن يَـٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِنِّىٓ أَنَا ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِين ٣٠

> But when he came to it, he was called from the bush in the sacred ground to the right side of the valley: “O Moses! It is truly I. I am Allah—the Lord of all worlds.

**Surah Al-Qasas 28:30**

---

Erhaben ist Allah Azzawajal davor, dass er ein Baum ist, vielmehr hat er durch den Baum gesprochen, so wie eine Person durch ein Handy sprechen kann, ohne selbst das Handy zu sein.

**Ibn Kathir (möge Allah ihm gnädig sein) sagt:**

> أَن يمُوسَى إِنِّى أَنَا اللَّهُ رَبُّ الْعَـلَمِينَ

> (O Musa! Verily, I am Allah, the Lord of all that exits!) meaning, 'the One Who is addressing you and speaking to you is the Lord of all that exits, the One Who does what He wills, the One apart from Whom there is no other god or lord, may He be exalted and sanctified, the One Who by His very nature, attributes, words and deeds is far above any resemblance to His creation, may He be glorified.
