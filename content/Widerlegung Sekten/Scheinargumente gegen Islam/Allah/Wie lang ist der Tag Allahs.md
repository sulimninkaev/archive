1000 Jahre: 22:47, 32:5
50000 Jahre: 70:4

**Quran 70:4 bezieht sich auf yawm al qiyamah:**

> تَعْرُجُ ٱلْمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ إِلَيْهِ فِى يَوْمٍۢ كَانَ مِقْدَارُهُۥ خَمْسِينَ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍۢ ٤

> ˹through which˺ the angels and the ˹holy˺ spirit will ascend to Him on a Day fifty thousand years in length.

[Surah Al-Ma'arij 70:4]

Dies wird deutlich, wenn man den Rest der Surah liest, in der übernatürliche Dinge beschrieben werden. 

**Ibn Abi Hatim recorded from Ibn 'Abbas that he said concerning the Ayah,**

> فِى يَوْمٍ كَانَ مِقْدَارُهُ خَمْسِينَ أَلْفَ سَنَةٍ

> (in a Day the measure whereof is fifty thousand years.) "It is the Day of Judgement."
